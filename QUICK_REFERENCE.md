# 🚀 Quick Reference - Executive Dashboard Changes

## What Changed?

### 1. Bar Charts Now Have Different Heights ✅
- **Where**: Therapeutic Specializations section (right side)
- **What**: Bars for Mental Health, Rheumatology, etc. are now different sizes
- **Why**: Better visual distinction between therapeutic areas

### 2. Map Locations Are Clickable ✅
- **Where**: National Clinical Footprint map (left side)
- **What**: Click any of the 3 center markers (New Delhi, Jaipur, Goa)
- **Shows**: Center name, address, subjects, trials, and performance metrics

### 3. Safety Incidents Are Now Viewable ✅
- **Where**: Critical Safety Incidents KPI card (red card, count: 3)
- **What**: Click the card to see incident details
- **Shows**: All 3 incidents with escalation levels and severity

---

## How to Use

### View Different Research Centers
1. Look for the map on the left
2. Click any location marker
3. A modal pops up with center details
4. Read the information
5. Click X or outside to close

### Check Safety Incident Status
1. Find the red "Critical Safety Incidents" card
2. Click on it (says "Click to view escalation status")
3. Modal opens showing incident #1
4. Click Incident #2 or #3 buttons to see others
5. Look at escalation level indicator (circles)
6. Close by clicking X or outside

### Analyze Trial Data
1. Look at the bar chart on the right
2. Notice bars have different heights now
3. Taller bars = more trials in that area
4. Use this for planning decisions
